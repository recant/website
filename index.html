GRANT PROPOSAL / EXPERIMENTAL DESIGN2026

A virus designed to selectively clear senescent cells

I designed a proposal for a modular viral senolytic: target entry through uPAR, restrict expression with a GLB1-linked promoter, then test siRNA payloads against senescent-cell anti-apoptotic pathway genes. The point was to stack independent layers of selectivity instead of trusting a single marker.

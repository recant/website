Viral vectors for selective senescent-cell clearance

A proposal to test whether multiple layers of biological selectivity can make senescent-cell killing safer: uPAR-biased viral entry, GLB1-driven transgene expression, and systematic screening of 18 SCAP-associated gene knockdowns for senescence-selective apoptosis.

Presentation

Machine learning optimization of a simple rule for predicting the sign of optical rotation of chiral compounds

Presenting at ACS Fall 2026. The project uses a Reaxys-derived dataset of approximately 13,000 single-stereocenter compounds to optimize an interpretable substituent-ranking heuristic, validate it out of sample, and map where the rule is most and least reliable.

Machine learning optimization of a simple rule for predicting the sign of optical rotation of chiral compounds

A large-scale test of an interpretable shortcut for optical rotation: optimize a substituent-ranking rule on a Reaxys-derived dataset of roughly 13,000 one-stereocenter compounds, validate it out of sample, and map where the heuristic does and does not work.

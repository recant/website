proposal

Selective viral targeting and clearance of senescent cells

A proposed strategy for selectively killing senescent cells using uPAR-targeted viral infection, GLB1-driven cargo expression, and siRNA knockdown of candidate senescent-cell anti-apoptotic genes.

proposal ·

The proposal has three aims: test uPAR as an infection target, test the GLB1 promoter for senescence-selective expression, and rank 18 SCAP-associated genes by how strongly their knockdown induces apoptosis in senescent versus non-senescent cells.
